Coding & Pizza Night

Category: Technology

Date and Time: September 24, 2026 at 6:00 PM

Location: Science Building, Room 201

Coding & Pizza Night is a relaxed event where students can meet other people interested in technology. Students can work on small coding activities, ask questions, and enjoy pizza together.

About the Event

This event is designed for students who want to practice coding and meet other students with similar interests. You do not need to be an experienced programmer to attend.

Students can bring their own laptop and work individually or with a small group. There will also be students available to help with basic questions.

What to Expect

  • Small beginner-friendly coding activities
  • Opportunities to work with other students
  • Free pizza and drinks
  • Time to meet students interested in technology

Event Schedule

  • 6:00 PM: Check-in and introductions
  • 6:15 PM: Coding activities begin
  • 7:00 PM: Pizza break
  • 7:30 PM: Group coding challenge
  • 8:30 PM: Event ends

Accessibility and Participation

The event room is wheelchair accessible. Students of all experience levels are welcome. If you need additional accommodations, contact the event organizer before the event.
